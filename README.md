# God's Eye

God's Eye is a Windows desktop tool designed to support Uttarakhand Police with
public safety work. It brings camera feeds and operator controls together in
one clear workspace, helping teams monitor busy locations, share situational
awareness, and coordinate a timely response.

## What it is for

The goal of God's Eye is to help police teams make better use of camera feeds
during everyday operations and large public gatherings. A focused view of
multiple feeds can support crowd management, help operators notice unusual
changes in crowd movement, and give teams more context when responding to an
incident.

God's Eye is also intended to support missing-person search workflows by helping
operators review available camera feeds and organize observations. In
combination with effective on-the-ground response, these tools aim to support
crime prevention, faster awareness, and safer public spaces across Uttarakhand.

The system is intended to assist police personnel, who remain responsible for
reviewing information and deciding on an appropriate response.

## At a glance

- **Find** and **Track** workspaces for focused camera-feed review.
- A camera preview, feed list, command panel, and results area.
- A clean Windows-style interface with workspace settings and quick hints.
- A workflow designed with crowd safety, anomaly awareness, and missing-person
  response in mind.
